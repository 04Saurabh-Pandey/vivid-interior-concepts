About Us

Company Overview:

Founded in 2013 as Interiors Concept and incorporated as a Private Limited Company on 29 June 2016, Vivid Interiors Concept is a leading interior design and renovation firm based in Noida, serving clients across Delhi.

Under the leadership of Mr. Sanjay Pandey, the company has built a strong reputation for design excellence, quality execution, and client-focused solutions. We specialize in creating customized interior spaces that reflect individual style while ensuring functionality and aesthetic appeal.

Supported by a team of experienced and creative professionals, we deliver projects with precision, flexibility, and timely execution. Our expertise spans residential, commercial, healthcare, educational, and retail interiors.

Equipped with advanced design tools such as 3ds Max and AutoCAD, and backed by over a decade of industry experience, Vivid Interiors Concept is committed to delivering high-quality interiors at competitive prices, ensuring complete client satisfaction.



Quality Assurance:

To deliver superior interior design solutions, we place strong emphasis on using reliable, durable, and high-quality materials. We maintain strategic tie-ups with leading manufacturers, enabling us to source premium products while ensuring competitive pricing for our clients.

All materials procured undergo strict quality checks to ensure that only approved and standard-compliant products are used in our projects. Our experienced and trustworthy supervisors closely monitor every stage of execution, paying equal attention to material quality, workmanship, and final finishes.

Our team of dedicated and skilled interior designers carefully analyzes site conditions, client requirements, and budget considerations. This systematic approach allows us to deliver tailored commercial and residential interior solutions that align with the client’s vision, lifestyle, and personality.
